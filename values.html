Core
values.

01

Time is your most valuable currency. Spend it wisely.

02

Create, don't just consume.

03

Pivot quickly and fail forward.

04

Joy is an act of resistance. the world is not designed for you to feel it — so be fiercely, deliberately, unapologetically joyful.

05

Stay authentic to who you truly are. Authenticity is infectious too — it inspires people to dare to be themselves.

06

Go quick and scrappy and iterate from there. Never work on something in isolation.

07

Less talking, more listening.

08

In a world optimised for speed and performance, empathy is the most radical thing you can lead with. stay human.

09

"A well characterized need is the DNA of a great invention." — Josh Makower

11

Stubbornness will kill connections, possibilities and your own happiness. Be agile and flexible — in life, with yourself and with others.

12

Comparison is where dreams go to die. Everyone has their own timeline. You do you.

13

Be kind and pay it forward. Something as simple as a smile could shift someone's day.
