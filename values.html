Core
values.

1.Time is your most valuable currency. Spend it wisely in ways that align with you.

2.Pivot quickly and fail forward.

3.Fall flat on your face, have a good cry about it, then get back up and try again tomorrow.

4.go quick and scrappy and iterate from there. Never work on something in isolation.

5.Comparison is where dreams go to die. Everyone has their own timeline. You do you.

6.Stay authentic to who you truly are. (don't let people tell you who to be)

7.Embarrassment doesn't exist if you don't allow it.

8.In a world optimised for speed and performance, empathy is the most radical thing you can lead with. stay human!!

9.Stubbornness will kill connections, possibilities and your own happiness. Be agile and flexible: in life, with yourself and with others.

10.Create, don't just consume.

11.We're only on this planet for a little while. Enjoy the fucking process.

12.Less talking, more listening. And ask questions!

13.Be kind and pay it forward when you feel you can.

14.“A well characterized need is the DNA of a great invention,” Josh Makower, a true inspiration
